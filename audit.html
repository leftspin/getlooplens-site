What it checks

Threads from the last 30 days that are at least 2 days old: whether each is a real customer inquiry, and whether the business replied within your threshold.

What it returns

A dashboard list of flagged threads, oldest first, each with an evidence page and a link to open it in Gmail.

What it does not do

It does not send email, draft responses, book appointments, change labels, archive mail, or become your CRM.
