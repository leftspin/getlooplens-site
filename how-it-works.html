Operational model

LoopLens watches for waiting customers, not workflows to take over.

It detects when the latest meaningful movement came from the customer and the thread has waited too long without a human response.

1. Read the thread state

Sender, latest inbound message, time waited, recipients, and whether there has been a reply.

2. Decide if it is stale

Elapsed time and thread context decide whether this is normal waiting or probable lead rot.

3. Show the evidence

The alert explains why it fired so the team can trust it before acting.

Detection should feel boringly legible.

No mystery automation. No customer-facing AI. Just a clear signal that a real person should look at the thread.
