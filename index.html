See where replies are waiting.

Start with a read-only audit of the inbox. You get a grade, the waiting threads behind it, and the safety boundary before LoopLens ever watches continuously.

1. Inspect read-only Gmail

LoopLens gets enough visibility to inspect inbound threads, not permission to speak for you or reorganize your mailbox.

2. Find stale inbound replies

It looks for customer or sales conversations where the latest meaningful message came from the customer and nobody has answered.

3. Report with evidence

Your team sees why the thread matters, how long it waited, and who should look at it in Gmail.

The audit returns a report, not a guess.

The one-time audit should feel useful even when the inbox is clean. A clean result is an A+ report for today, plus a clear case for why continuous watching matters tomorrow.
