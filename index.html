See where replies are waiting.

Start with a free read-only audit of the last 30 days. You see the threads that waited too long and the evidence for each before you decide on daily scans.

1. Inspect read-only Gmail

LoopLens gets enough visibility to inspect inbound threads, not permission to speak for you or reorganize your mailbox.

2. Find stale inbound replies

AI picks out real customer inquiries. A fixed rule then flags any where the business has not replied within your threshold, 48 hours by default.

3. Show the evidence

Each flagged thread gets an evidence page with why it was flagged and a link to open it in Gmail.

The audit returns a list, not a guess.

Free Audit runs one scan. The result is either a list of flagged threads with evidence, or a plain statement that none need attention right now.
