Support

Talk to a person.

Email support@getlooplens.com with your question and the email address you use to sign in. A person reads every message and replies by email.

Google says LoopLens is unverified

LoopLens is in beta while Google completes its review of our Gmail access. Until that finishes, Google shows a warning that the app is not verified. To continue, choose Advanced, then Go to LoopLens. LoopLens only asks to read your Gmail; it cannot send, delete, label or move mail. During the beta Google limits LoopLens to 100 new users.

My Google Workspace admin blocks LoopLens

Some Google Workspace admins block apps that Google has not yet verified. If you see a message that your organization blocks the app, your admin needs to allow LoopLens in the Google Admin console under Security, Access and data control, API controls. Email us and we will send your admin the details they need.

Disconnecting Gmail

To stop LoopLens reading your Gmail right away, remove LoopLens in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services. Closing your LoopLens account also revokes its Google access. Removing an inbox in the app does not revoke that access, so use your Google Account to stop it.

Alerts

Alerts are part of paid plans and go only to the account owner, never to the people who emailed you. Alerts are sent by email to the owner's sign-in address and say how many conversations need attention so you can review them in the app; they do not include names or message text. Alerts are sent between 8 AM and 5 PM in your business time zone. Text alerts are coming later; when they are available, reply STOP to any LoopLens text to stop texts.

Billing and plans

Billing is monthly per business, through Stripe. Change or cancel your plan from Billing in the app. See pricing.

Closing your account

The account owner can close the account from the Account page by typing the business name to confirm. Scanning stops and LoopLens revokes its Google access for the inboxes still connected. A free account closes right away; a paid account closes at the end of the paid period. Account data is deleted 30 days after closure.

Privacy and your data

For questions or requests about your data, email support@getlooplens.com.

LoopLens watches inbox rot without sending, drafting, labeling, archiving, booking, or replacing Gmail.
