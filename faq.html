Quick answers

FAQ for cautious owners.

Short answers about access, alerts, clean inboxes, and what LoopLens never does.

Does LoopLens send email?

No. Humans reply in Gmail.

Does it replace Gmail, a CRM, or a shared inbox?

No. It watches for lead rot and reports evidence.

What if the audit finds nothing?

That is an A+ report for today. It still shows what was checked and why ongoing watching can catch tomorrow's miss.

Will it create noisy alerts?

Each alert explains why it fired, and teams can tune thresholds and exclusions.

What happens after login?

You connect Gmail read-only, review the audit result, and decide whether LoopLens should keep watching for stale inbound replies.
