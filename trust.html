Safety boundary

Read-only is the product promise.

LoopLens is designed for owners who want visibility without handing customer communication to another system.

No customer contact

LoopLens never sends, drafts, schedules, or replies.

No mailbox mutation

No labels, no archive changes, no routing changes, no CRM sync claims.

Owner-only alerts

Alerts go only to the account owner and say how many leads are flagged. They contain no names or message text.

Plain-English permissions

Enough access to inspect. Not enough authority to act.

LoopLens asks Google for read-only Gmail access. It reads message headers and Gmail's short message snippets to find threads waiting on a reply. It cannot send, delete, label, archive or move mail. You can remove its access at any time in your Google Account.
